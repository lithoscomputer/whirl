Lakeside cabin with sauna

Entire cabin in Ely, Minnesota · 4 guests · 2 bedrooms · 1 bath

About this place

A quiet cedar cabin on a private bay of Shagawa Lake. Paddle from the dock at sunrise, warm up in the wood-fired sauna, and end the day by the fire pit.

The cabin sits at the end of a gravel road, 10 minutes from town. The road is plowed in winter.

What this place offers

  • Wood-fired sauna
  • Two kayaks and a canoe
  • Wood stove
  • Full kitchen
  • Wifi
  • Free parking

Where you'll sleep

Main bedroom: 1 queen bed. Bunk room: 2 twin beds.
